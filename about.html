About Us

Everything you need to know:

Urban Barista was founded back in 2020 with a passion for coffee and community.
Just then it was a place where locals were able to visit. Later on in 2024 we decided to grow more and that was a year we officially launched our website.
The mission is to serve high-quality, ethically sourced products and services, while creating a welcoming space for everyone.
The vision was bigger than ever, being the go-to cafe that inspres connection and sustainability in our community.
